A mature longhorn man of noble, philosophical air. Malbleat is tall (almost 7′), graceful, slender, and has black fur with a silver nose. Dresses in refined silks, with a fashionable neck ruff. Renowned for his love of poetry and his elegance in the ballroom. Dabbles in matters occult.

Malbleat is the younger half-brother (to different mothers) of [[Lord Ramius]]. He forced Baron Hogwarsh to recognize his competing claim to nobility in the High Wold, reinstating House Gnarlgruff. Hogwarsh compromised between Ramius and Malbleat and split their family lands between the two of them.  Bad blood still exists.

Lives at Redwraith Manor with his many wives and servants. 

Not well liked by the residents of [[Lankshorn]], as he taxes them and does whatever he pleases.
