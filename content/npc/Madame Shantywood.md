Runs Chateau Shantywood, a casino on Shantywood Isle. 

Historically, Shantywood Isle is an independant political zone, not under the control of any nobility. This gives Madame Shantywood freedom to do as she pleases. 

As it turns out, what she pleases is to control the port of [[Dreg]], as well as involve herself in various interests in the surrounding area. 

Our heroes have been contracted by her to collect debts, and to secure a supply of magic crystals that she has need of. See [[20th Iggwylde, Shantywood Isle]], her formal offer of employment to them, passed to them through her butler [[Alphonse LeDuc]].

Her two daughters [[Ayla Shantywood]] and [[Heremine Shantywood]] work for her.
