**Lord Wrygott Gnarlgruff** (often simply referred to as Lord Gnarlgruff) is a significant figure in the history and lore of Dolmenwood, particularly among the longhorn nobility

Lord Malbleat hosts an annual festival called **The Hlerribuck** to commemorate Gnarlgruff’s life. 
## Identity and Historical Role

* **Ancestry:** He is a **distant ancestor** of the longhorn nobles [[Lord Malbleat]] and [[Lord Ramius]].
* Lord Malbleat's house, House Gnarlgruff, bears his name
* **Power and Profession:** Gnarlgruff was the **most powerful and revered longhorn of his day**, renowned as a **great longhorn sorcerer** and a practitioner of **dark and awful magicks**. He was also a **mighty necromancer**
* **Enemies:** He is considered an **old enemy** of the Drune

.

## Final Resting Place: 
After his death, Gnarlgruff was interred in the **Black Tomb**

This tomb is located in the depths of the Malbleat family crypt complex, the **Shadholme** (hex 0709). The entrance to the Black Tomb is flanked by two obsidian slabs inscribed in Caprice with "**the Black Tomb of Lord Wrygott Gnarl-gruff**"

**Laboratory:** Gnarlgruff practiced dire sorcery at a location now known as the Devil Goats' Glade
