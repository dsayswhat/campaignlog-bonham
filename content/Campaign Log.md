## 21 Iggwyld

Overcast, muggy, thunder but no rain

Team goes shopping

* pick up magic dagger for Syd
* Hendrik ordered a moon-forged polearm which illuminates when he says its name. Name is 'Orion'. available 31 Iggwyld 
* Hendrik repaired armor, available 24 Iggwyld

Visited [[Sydewich Maldwort]] at the Man of Gold Apothecary. He's looking at a sample of the horn taken from the creature in the smokehouse, results on 24 Iggwyld

Party headed to the Shadholme, where the Hlerribuck festival is taking place. Besides the fair in field next to the lodge, there's a fancier party inside the lodge itself, built over the crypts where Breggle nobles have been buried for centuries.  

Spoke to some nobles in the Shadholme Lodge garden, including [[Hrannick Berklin]], who is organizing the festivities on behalf of [[Lord Malbleat]]. He took an interest in Runk's musical abilities and asked to hear him play later on that day, in case Lord Malbleat might be interested in having him entertain in the lodge.

As the day progresses, Syd met up with a fortune teller who told him "The living are imprisoned, and the dead walk! You'll see - you will see!".

Syd followed her and engaged her in conversation as she prepared a meal and drank a pot of tea that supports her fortune telling abilities. After a small sip, Syd also saw glimpses of what might be, with the fortune teller slain and the festival tents burning and blowing away in the middle of a lightning storm. 

Later, Runk was playing for a crowd of people, when the local guards asked for his permit. Syd attempted to bribe them, but they suggested a more appropriate bribe might be to buy a round of drinks for the guard post at the Shadholme.

Then Berklin and Malbleat arrived to listen to Runk. They were pleased with his performance, and Malbleat asked Runk to compose a tune, to be played at the party tomorrow night. This song should be an epic ballad telling the story of [[Lord Gnarlgruff]], Malbleat's ancestor. 

Runk should return tomorrow ready to rehearse his song with [[Emelda Wishorn]], who has apparently been here preparing to perform for the last several days. Folks in Lankshorn were saying she'd gone missing, but apparently here she is???

"Embala of the Husk, lake mother dream sender,
