Dated 20th of Iggwylde
From the desk of [[Alphonse LeDuc]], esq.

To the Esteemed Adventures,

I write to you with Shantywood’s compliments that I convey the following two commissions for your consideration. 

---

### First Commission: Collection of Outstanding Debt

Madame has a rather pressing matter of **outstanding gambling debt** that requires immediate attention. The individual in question is a grimalkin by the name of [[**Whimsy Bobblefrock**]], It is rumored that she has settled in Lankshorn, acting as the proprietor of Bogway's tavern and stable. 

Whimsy accrued a considerable debt at Chateau Shantywood, amounting to **5,000 gold pieces**. She has, unfortunately, proven most recalcitrant in settling her obligations, having previously **escaped imprisonment** for these very debts. Madame Shantywood, known for her rigorous approach to justice, is willing to accept an alternative payment should the gold prove elusive: **Whimsy Bobblefrock's left ear**.

For the successful retrieval of the aforementioned ear, Madame will grant your party **500 gold pieces**. Should you, however, manage to extract any sum beyond the **5,000 gold piece debt** from Ms. Bobblefrock, that excess amount will be considered your additional 'service fee'.

---

### Second Commission: Supply of Magical Crystals

In a matter of more esoteric interest, Madame Shantywood maintains a keen interest in **high-quality magical crystals**. She seeks a **steady supply** for her various pursuits, including aiding sorcerers like Myrrsian the Mutable who desires such crystals for powerful magical works. These rare and valuable specimens, rumored to be found within the Crystal Caves at Fog Lake, are integral to her extensive collections and arcane research.

Madame is prepared to offer generous compensation for suitable specimens. Her current rates for such materials, based on quality per pound, are as follows:

- **Good Quality Magic Crystal**: 50 gold pieces per pound.
- **Fine Quality Magic Crystal**: 200 gold pieces per pound.
- **Very Fine Quality Magic Crystal**: 750 gold pieces per pound.
- **Excellent Quality Magic Crystal**: 2,500 gold pieces per pound.

Please note that these specific per-pound valuations for magical crystals are offered as a guideline from Madame’s own estimation, as such precise rates are not universally codified in the mortal world. We anticipate a consistent flow of these valuable resources, and Madame trusts that your discernment in quality will match her own exacting standards.

---

Enclosed, please find a letter of credit which should cover living expenses and incidentals, to a  value of 750 silver pieces. It can be drawn upon in any settlement in the High Wold, the town of Prigwort or Castle Brackenwold. 

This letter of credit is good until the 1st of Lillypythe, 5 weeks from now, by which time Madame hopes you will have returned with proof of your success in one or both of your tasks.

Yours in service,

Alphonse LeDuc, Butler to Madame Chandilore Shantywood
