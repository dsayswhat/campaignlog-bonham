## High Wold

* Drowned town of Lankston, west on the Ditchway, edge of the forest
* Nightworms: If you travel east along the Ditchway, make sure to stay off the road at night. The nearby woods are teeming with giant, red worms that crave human flesh (0809). True.
* The Jaunty Horn, situated on the ditchway between Lankshorn and Dreg, is operated by Jessibelle Nag, former adventurer and arm-wrestling champion
* Something feels weird north and south in the area around the Jaunty Horn, as if an evil presence is watching you
* the Pot-head fairies: The mounds in the woods to the southeast ([[0810]]) are haunted by a rabble of fairies. Their chief is a bowl-headed scamp known as [[Thraggletwist]], who spies upon travellers along [[The Ditchway]]. Partly true: The fairy’s name is Thrattlewhit. He is in love with a local Braithmaid, whom he besottedly follows, but does not spy on travellers.

## Dreg

* Brother Hogsbeard and his gang of yellow-smocks - religious zealot
* Something has been changing in Dreg over the last 5 years. People aren't kind to one another anymore, and Brother Hogsbeard wasn't always this mean
* Madame Shantywood runs the town, more or less, controlling the docks and the casino on Shantywood Isle.
* The mermaid's arms tavern is a safe place to stay. But some say it's haunted?
* Say hello to Mother Hadlewreathe at the church of St. Wick, from Mallowyn and Trelade Occland, twin sisters and proprietors at the House of the Harridwn.

## Lankshorn

* Lankshorn bladesmith: The bladesmith, Joremey, practices a magical smith-craft taught to him by fairies. Partly true: It was taught to him by his father (of elf ancestry)
*  The Hlerribuck, celebrating the noble ancestors of Lord Malbleat, takes place on the 22 of this month, which is a few days from now.
* A band of goblin merchants is due to meet your party at the Hlerribuck festival.
